Explore your birth chart, numerology and daily panchang with AastroAstra.

YOUR BIRTH CHART
Enter your birth details to explore kundali charts, planetary positions, nakshatra and dasha information. An unknown birth time makes some interpretations approximate.

YOUR NUMBERS
Explore your birth number, destiny number, name number and Lo Shu grid. Compare profiles and read the explanations behind the results.

MATCHING AND PANCHANG
Compare birth details with kundali matching and explore the daily panchang for your location.

AI EXPLANATIONS AND PHOTO READINGS
Ask questions about your chart, or choose a palm or face reading. Photo readings require consent before photos and relevant reading details are sent to an external AI provider. Palm photos remain on your device for history and retries. AastroAstra does not store face photos or use them to identify you. You can report AI content inside the app.

LANGUAGES AND ACCESS
The interface supports twelve languages: English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Bengali, Punjabi, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Spanish and Chinese. Sign in with the code or link in your email.
